MAM Affiliate Member Application Process

NO VOTING RIGHTSVOTING RIGHTS
01NEW APPLICATION
NO VOTING RIGHTS
  • Affiliated membership is open to clubs, associations or any organisation that the Executive Committee deems fit and relevant.
  • Each application shall be nominated by an Affiliate and seconded by another Affiliate.

Procedure for application

  • New applicants must submit a written application for membership to the Secretary General and furnish the following documents:-
    1. Completed Application Form
    2. Club Profile with list of members
    3. Certificate of Registration with PJS (for Clubs/Associations - Form 3)
    4. Certificate of Incorporation (for Companies - Form 9)
    5. Latest Form 24, 44 & 49 (for Companies & Circuit Owners)
    6. Latest Bank Statement
    7. 3 years Report of Past Events, Programs & Activities (if any)
  • Application review process takes 3 - 5 working days & the results will be notified via email.
  • Certificate of Membership will only be issued upon receipt of full payment of the Affiliation Fee.
02PROBATION (1 YEAR)
NO VOTING RIGHTS
  • New Clubs/Associations shall undergo a one (1) year probationary period commencing from the date of acceptance of the terms & conditions and payment of the Affiliation Fee (“Probationary Period”).
  • During the Probationary Period, new Clubs/Associations must organise and submit a written report to MAM on at least three (3) motorsports activities such as racing and/or activities promoting motorsports through exhibitions, seminars, classes or training (“Motorsports Activities”).
  • All Motorsports Activities organised must be in strict compliance with the Constitution, relevant laws, rules, regulations and guidelines set by the authorities and MAM.
  • New Clubs/Associations that do not have any track record of organizing races shall be strictly monitored by MAM.
  • Upon the expiry of the Probationary Period, the Executive Committee shall evaluate the new Clubs/Association’s performance and has the absolute discretion to either:-
    1. Approve the membership to Full Affiliated status; OR
    2. Extend the probation period.
03AFFILIATE MEMBER
VOTING RIGHTS
  • New Clubs/Associations awarded full affiliate status is deemed a Voting Member and is required to organize, conduct and maintain a minimum of three (3) Motorsports Activities per calendar year, failing which its status as a full affiliated member may be reviewed by the Executive Committee.
  • All Motorsports Activities organised must be in strict compliance with the Constitution, relevant laws, rules, regulations and guidelines set by the authorities and MAM.
  • Each member must renew their annual membership and pay requisite fee on/before 31st January each year. Failure to meet the deadline shall result to an automatic suspension of its membership unless MAM determines and decides otherwise.
  • Each member is encouraged to propose new members to MAM.
