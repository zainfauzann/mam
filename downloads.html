03

Licence Applications

Novice (C), National (A/B) and International (A/B/C) licences for drivers and riders, plus annual Team Entrant licences. Applicants under 18 need parental consent.

  1. 01Complete the formCompetition Licence Application Form 2026, or apply online.
  2. 02Medical check-upPrint the Medical Examination Form and take it to any clinic, with ECG.
  3. 03Supporting documentsPassport photo, IC copy, blood group card, medical report, proof of MAM-affiliated club membership.
  4. 04PaymentPersatuan Sukan Bermotor Malaysia · Maybank 5623 5753 4201.
  5. 05SubmitWalk in at the MAM office, 1st Floor Paddock Office, SIC, or email the Secretariat.
  6. 06CollectMAM will contact you within about 7 days of completion.
