FIM and FIA inscription fees for the three higher levels are invoiced separately when received.

  • Club events need at least one steward, at RM1,500 per steward per day.
  • National and international events need two stewards. The fees above cover two stewards for two days.
  • Each extra day costs RM1,500 per steward. With two stewards, a 3-day event is RM6,000 + RM3,000 = RM9,000 and a 4-day event is RM6,000 + RM6,000 = RM12,000.
  • MAM may change these fees and will give its reasons.

Competition licence fees 2026
